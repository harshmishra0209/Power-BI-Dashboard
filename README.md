# Power-BI-Dashboard
This repository contains a **Power BI Sales Dashboard** built for analyzing Motorola’s sales performance across regions, products, and time periods.  
The dashboard provides powerful insights to support business decisions, product strategy, and revenue growth forecasting.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

The Motorola Sales Dashboard focuses on:

- Tracking overall sales performance
- Monitoring product-wise revenue
- Identifying top-performing regions
- Understanding customer buying trends
- Analyzing month-wise sales growth
- Visualizing KPIs for leadership reporting

This project demonstrates hands-on experience with **Power BI, DAX, data modeling, sales analytics, and business intelligence**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Provide clear visibility into Motorola’s sales trends  
- Improve decision-making for product and sales teams  
- Identify high-performing regions and weak markets  
- Analyze sales contribution by product category  
- Deliver board-level KPIs in a single report
